# Workstream: WS-C Product Review and Closeout

## Objective

Require product and mixed outcomes to demonstrate the intended job on representative real data and receive human product-quality judgment before closure, while giving infrastructure an honest technical closeout path.

## Owned Files/Areas

- Representative-demo and product-review evidence schema/template/update shape
- Quality and closeout skills, runbooks, checklists, and completion summary
- Project/task close and parent-rollup preconditions
- Infrastructure closeout semantics and first felt-value restatement
- Focused evidence freshness, human-review, critical-finding, automation-only, and no-write-on-failure tests
- WS-C task evidence and decisions

## Dependencies

- T-007 consumes the value/demo vocabulary from T-001 and trace design from T-004.
- T-008 consumes re-boil/lifecycle semantics from T-005 and the evidence contract from T-007.
- Kotlin/adversarial fixtures in WS-D consume the completed closure boundary.

## Risks

- A checklist or numeric score can produce the appearance of taste without judgment.
- Screenshots can prove that a useless screen rendered, and real-data labels can be asserted without exercising representative complexity.
- Local reviewer display names are accountable provenance, not authenticated identity.
- Parent auto-rollups can bypass a closeout check if mutation paths are not centralized.

## Handoff Criteria

- Product/mixed closeout runs every representative demo step on named representative data/build evidence.
- A named human records intended-job completion and critical findings for information architecture, copy, contextual actions, data usefulness, and interaction quality.
- Any failed demo step or unresolved critical product finding blocks closure even when all automated checks pass.
- Infrastructure closeout proves its technical promise and restates the first felt-value boundary without claiming product acceptance.
- Every close/rollup/direct-validation path shares the same freshness and failure-without-write semantics.
